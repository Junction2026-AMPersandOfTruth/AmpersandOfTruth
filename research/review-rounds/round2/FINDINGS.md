## Clearly works

- **Landing page clarity and real-pipeline cutover (`01-landing-desktop.png`):** Clean Wikipedia-inspired hierarchy (`What was pushed into Wikipedia?`, crisp 2-px corners, primary blue `Investigate` CTA, 7 topic chips, collapsed filters, 3-step explainer, CC BY-SA 4.0 footer) with zero sample-data banner now that `Investigations:Source=Pipeline` is live.
- **Overview speed, retrieval quality, and Round 1 layout fixes (`03-overview-desktop.png`):** Warmed real-pipeline results for *Civil War 1918* load in `< 3 s` with 25 real incidents across core articles (*Finnish Civil War*, *Red Guards*, *Battle of Oulu*, *Senate of Finland*), compact `Signal ▾` popover, English + Finnish rail titles with sparklines and contested-period notes, `View on Wikipedia ↗` links on every card, and `▸ Removed edits we did not flag (14)` at the bottom.
- **Incident detail evidence highlights, context folding, and assessment framing (`04-incident-desktop.png`, `05-diff-toggle-desktop.png`):** Soft blue/amber diff spans with dashed evidence underlines link `AUTOMATED ASSESSMENT` quotes (`“Suomen sisällissotaa”` → `“Suomen vapaussotaa”`) directly to the exact words in `WHAT CHANGED`, unchanged prose folds cleanly (`⋯ 24 unchanged lines`), `English headline: Machine translation` sits above the `<h1>`, and `This looks wrong` + `Copy link` actions sit cleanly below `Related`.
- **Fast, structured Article timeline & `.aot-wikitable` (`06-article-desktop.png`):** Previously stuck on blank grey skeleton bars in Round 1 (`R1-02`), `/articles/{pageId}` now renders in `< 0.3 s` with stacked registered/anonymous monthly bars, `Most contested` auto-focus (`Apr 2019 – May 2019 (2 removals)`), `Read as of a date`, and a crisp Wikipedia-style revision table with UTC timestamps and `View change ↗` links.
- **Out-of-scope topic guard and recent-search persistence (`09-climate-change-desktop.png`):** Submitting `"climate change"` stays on Landing with a calm, honest inline `EmptyState` (`Our data covers the history of Finland. ‘climate change’ is outside it.`) and one-click recovery chips instead of a false "zero disputed insertions" result, while `Recent in this browser: Mannerheim` persists below.
- **Mobile Overview hierarchy and legible responsive timeline (`01-landing-phone.png`, `03-overview-phone.png`):** Collapsing `▸ ARTICLES (10)` and `▸ Filters` below `992px` plus the dedicated stacked mobile SVG timeline puts the top `IncidentCard` inside the first `390×844` screen with crisp year ticks and zero horizontal overflow.
