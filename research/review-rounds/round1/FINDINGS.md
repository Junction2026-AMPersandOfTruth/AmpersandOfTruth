## What Clearly Works

- **Calm Wikipedia-native visual identity (`01-landing-desktop.png`, `03-overview-desktop.png`):** Crisp 2-px square corners, zero drop shadows, serif titles/claims with horizontal rules paired against system sans UI, `surface-sunken` infobox sidebars, and non-alarmist pale tier tints make the app feel immediately credible next to Wikipedia.
- **Self-teaching non-hue tier grammar and unflagged-edits transparency (`03-overview-desktop.png`):** Pairing 1–3 pips (`▮▯▯`, `▮▮▯`, `▮▮▮`) with distinct timeline shapes (hollow circle, diamond, square), a `Sample incidents: not findings` banner, and the `Removed edits we did not flag (38)` disclosure builds strong user trust without relying on red/green "guilt" colours.
- **Direct evidence linkage and epistemic humility on Incident detail (`04-incident-desktop.png`):** Soft blue insertion tint + dashed evidence underline directly connecting `AUTOMATED ASSESSMENT` quotes to the exact span in `WHAT CHANGED`, explicit `Uncertainty:` notes, UTC timestamps (`31 Jan 2019, 19:51 UTC`) + `diff ↗` links on every event row, and *"Removed does not mean false, and kept does not mean true"* copy exemplify the P0.7 standard.
- **Jargon-free, honest Methodology page (`10-methodology-desktop.png`):** The 5-box inline SVG pipeline diagram, the three `Evidence levels` cards embedding live `TierBadge`s, explicit *"Left out altogether"* exclusions, and candid `Limitations` + `CC BY-SA 4.0` attribution avoid all banned terms (`revert`, `tier`, `LLM`, intent accusations).
- **Mobile Incident column interleaving and zero horizontal overflow (`04-incident-phone.png`, `report.md`):** Across all 20 walk steps on desktop and `390×844` phone there were `0` console errors, `0` failed requests, and `0` horizontal scroll overflows, and Incident detail on phone smartly hides `Side by side` diff mode while slotting `Why this is flagged` + `AUTOMATED ASSESSMENT` right after `WHAT CHANGED` ahead of the 14-row `Sequence of events`.
