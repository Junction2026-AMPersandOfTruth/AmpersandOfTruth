Walking `category_link` recursively from `Suomen historia` yields **2,340 distinct categories** (maximum depth **7**) covering **all 19,285 Finnish articles** (`article.lang = 'fi'`) present in the corpus. ### Honest overall verdict (Q-R)

> **Verdict:** **Roughly 16% of substantive (`>= 50` B) reverted runs look like deliberate political or historical narrative-pushing** (rising to **~22%** if subtle date/fact fabrications in `factual-manipulation` are included). Most substantive reverts across the broad `Suomen historia` tree are still ordinary encyclopedia maintenance — **26%** good-faith unsourced additions, **24%** vandalism/graffiti, **6%** editing accidents, and **22%** administrative/copyright/category/non-political reverts (notably **7%** from block-evasion rollbacks alone). **However, narrative-pushing edits are ~4x larger (`+439 B` vs `+106 B`) and survive ~18x longer (`42 min` median vs `2.3 min`) than graffiti** — meaning raw revert volume is noisy, whereas filtering for longer, slower-reverted prose diffs on historically or geopolitically contested pages surfaces genuine ideological tug-of-wars.

## 4. Caveats & Pitch Guidance for Engineering / Demo

1. **Raw revert counts ≠ narrative contestation:** Only **~16%** of substantive (`>= 50` B) reverted runs across the scope are strict political/historical narrative-pushing (~22% including silent `factual-manipulation`). **Do not pitch the app as "every revert is disinformation."** Pitch it as surfacing the **high-signal minority of contested historical/geopolitical framing** hidden inside ~80% routine maintenance and graffiti.
2. **Simple heuristics separate narrative battles from graffiti:** In Q-R, `narrative/POV-pushing` runs had a median size of **+439 B** and median survival of **42 min** (with topic-shortlist examples regularly surviving **6 hours to 38 days**), whereas `vandalism/graffiti` had median **+106 B** and **2.3 min** survival. Filtering candidate runs to **`net_bytes_added >= 100`**, **`survival >= 15 min`**, and excluding Part-A pitfalls (`R.bytes = 0`, self-reverts, `*bot*` reverters) strips out most profanity and accidents cheaply before any LLM/vector step.
3. **Sockpuppet block-evasion (`WPK`) inflates `other`:** About **7%** of all substantive reverts in Q-R (`#15, #16, #20, #21, #40, #44`, plus `Hippophaë` `#57`) are administrative rollbacks of prolific banned editors whose edits are often structurally normal or sourced prose on 1940s elections and geography. Filter revert comments matching `WPK` / `estoa kiertävä` / `väärinkäyttösuodatin` so administrative rollbacks aren't mislabelled as contested history.
4. **Identity-revert blind spot:** Exact-SHA1 reverts only capture edits that were *completely* undone back to an earlier revision `P`. Subtle framing compromises where a second editor *rewrites* rather than reverts an ideological insertion are invisible to SHA1 identity matching — an honest limitation worth noting in the demo.
5. **PK fetch budget compliance:** Across both rounds combined, total `revision_text` fetches were **284 PK rows** (`200` for Q-R + `84` for Q-T + `0` in Round 2), strictly within the `<= 300` PK limit.
